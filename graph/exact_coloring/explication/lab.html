Results Analysis

Beyond simple verification, this laboratory allows observing the degradation of the gap between heuristics and the theoretical optimum.

On large instances, the value of χ becomes unreachable by brute force. The gap between the worst greedy and the best algorithm (RLF/DSATUR) then becomes the true indicator of the filter's power used. The validity checker ensures that this gap is measured on valid colorings — an undetected conflict would skew the entire analysis.

A coloring is only valuable if it is verified: counting the colors of an invalid coloring is measuring emptiness.
